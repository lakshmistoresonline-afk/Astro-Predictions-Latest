# Astrovision Varga (Divisional Charts) Audit

## Supported Divisional Charts
- **D1 (Rashi)**: Primary natal chart.
- **D2 (Hora)**: Wealth and planetary division.
- **D3 (Drekkana)**: Siblings and courage.
- **D9 (Navamsa)**: Soul purpose, marriage, and inner strength.
- **D10 (Dasamsa)**: Career, profession, and public standing.
- **D16 (Shodasamsa)**: Vehicles and general happiness.
- **D20 (Vimsamsa)**: Spiritual progress and devotion.
- **D24 (Siddhamsa)**: Education and higher learning.
- **D27 (Nakshatramsa)**: Strengths and weaknesses.
- **D30 (Trimsamsa)**: Misfortunes and health.
- **D60 (Shastiamsha)**: Past life karma and overall destiny.
