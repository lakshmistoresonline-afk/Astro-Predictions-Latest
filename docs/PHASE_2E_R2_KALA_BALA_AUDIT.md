# Phase 2E-R2 Kala Bala Audit

## Findings & Remediation
In Phase 2E-R1, Kala Bala was audited as incomplete because it only calculated 3 subcomponents (Nathonnatha, Paksha, Ayana).

In Phase 2E-R2, Kala Bala was expanded to include all 8 subcomponents prescribed by classical Parashari convention:
1. **Nathonnatha Bala** (0-60 shashtiamsas)
2. **Paksha Bala** (0-60 shashtiamsas)
3. **Ayana Bala** (0-60 shashtiamsas)
4. **Tribhaga Bala** (0 or 60 shashtiamsas based on 1/3rd day/night partition)
5. **Vara Bala** (0 or 45 shashtiamsas for Day Lord)
6. **Hora Bala** (0 or 60 shashtiamsas for Hour Lord)
7. **Masa Bala** (0 or 30 shashtiamsas for Month Lord)
8. **Varsha Bala** (0 or 15 shashtiamsas for Year Lord)

**PASS**. Zero placeholders remain in Kala Bala.
