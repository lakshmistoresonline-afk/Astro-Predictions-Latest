# Phase 2E Ashtakavarga Convention & Rule Provenance

## 1. Executive Summary
This document specifies the exact classical convention utilized for calculating Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV) and Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) points.

## 2. Selected Canonical Convention
- **CANONICAL TRADITION**: Maharishi Parashara (*Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra*, Ch. 66 "Ashtakavarga").
- **CONTRIBUTORS**: 7 Classical Planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) + Ascendant (Lagna). Total 8 contributors. Rahu and Ketu are excluded from contributing to standard BAV matrices under classical Parashari rules.
- **HOUSES**: Computed relative to the sign placement (Rashi) of each contributor. House counting is inclusive (the sign occupied by the planet/ascendant is House 1).
- **SAV SUMMATION**: SAV totals are strictly the arithmetic sum of the 7 planetary BAV matrices for each sign. (In BPHS standard SAV, Ascendant BAV is not natively aggregated into the SAV score, leaving a canonical universal total of exactly 337 bindus).
