# Astrovision Rule Engine Specification

## 1. Yoga Rules
- **Gajakesari Yoga**: Jupiter in a Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from Moon.
- **Budhaditya Yoga**: Conjunction of Sun and Mercury in the same sign/house.
- **Viparita Raja Yoga**: Lords of 6, 8, or 12 inhabiting 6, 8, or 12.

## 2. Dosha Rules
- **Manglik Dosha**: Mars placed in 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from Lagna/Moon.
- **Sade Sati**: Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st, or 2nd house from natal Moon.
